The Harvard Kennedy School — Cambridge Operating Plan:Enabling the Mission Through Faculty-Centered Operations

Faculty Lifecycle & Sponsored-Research Operations Platform

Proposed operating philosophy. Lead through consensus and alignment, without relying on formal authority: connect strategy, people, analytics and systems, and leave each decision with the office that holds it.

The program. Appointments, promotions and grant workflows run on email and spreadsheets, so cycle times are long and status is unclear. The proposal: tracked workflows from search to offer and proposal to award, with cycle times and compliance visible to leaders.

Alignment and governance. Each workstream maps to 1 priorities stated in the posting. Any connection to systems of record would need authorized access and a governance review. Benefits are measured against baselines set at G1, not asserted here.
